LogFlightPro — Professional Pilot Logbook Log smarter. Fly further. LogFlightPro is an advanced digital flight logbook designed specifically for professional pilots. Whether you're flying for an airline, instructing, or building hours — LogFlightPro makes logging every flight faster, more accurate, and fully compliant with EASA regulations. Built by pilots, for pilots. Key Features: Effortless Flight Logging • Smart input fields with automatic time calculations • Dynamic crew management and role tagging (PIC, SIC, PM, PF) • Autofill for aircraft type and airport details • HH:MM time format and decimal support Night Time Detection • Automatic detection of night takeoffs, landings, and night time • Based on precise sunrise/sunset data at departure and arrival locations Import & OCR Support • Import previous flights from PDF or airline roster • Built-in OCR for scanned documents EASA-Compliant PDF Export • Generate beautiful A4 landscape logbooks • Fully matches EASA format — ready for printing or submission • Includes digital signature and bolded totals Experience Tracking • Visual summaries of PIC, Co-Pilot, Dual, Instructor, and Simulator time • Separate tracking for Single Pilot SE/ME, Multi Pilot, Night, IFR • All flight times shown in HH:MM format for precision Airport & Aircraft Intelligence • ICAO-based auto-fill with country flag, airport name, and location • Tail number lookup with automatic aircraft type detection Secure & Private • Face ID and password protection • Local encrypted storage and secure backup options • Full iCloud, Firebase, or Supabase integration (optional) Designed for All Professional Pilots Whether you're logging flights for airline recency, tracking instructor hours, or exporting records for authority review — LogFlightPro adapts to your workflow. • Commercial pilots • Flight instructors • Simulator instructors • Student pilots • General aviation and time-builders • Freelance or contract pilots needing audit-ready records Why LogFlightPro? • No subscriptions required to get started • Built for long-term career pilots — not just students • Matches real logbook layouts used by airlines and EASA auditors • Regular updates, full offline access, and beautiful modern UI Your Logbook, Reimagined.
